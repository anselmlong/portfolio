# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineers, collaborators, classmates, and curious readers visit this portfolio to understand Anselm Long's work, writing, technical range, and personality. They are usually scanning quickly first, then deciding whether to read deeper, contact him, or inspect a specific project.

## Product Purpose

This site presents Anselm's projects, experience, writing, photos, resume, and AI-powered portfolio chat in one coherent personal surface. Success means visitors can quickly form a credible impression of his engineering taste, find proof through concrete work, and move naturally into reading, chatting, or contacting him.

## Brand Personality

Curious, technical, candid. The voice should feel like a builder explaining what he tried, what worked, and what he learned, without sanding away the personal edge that makes the writing recognizable.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page polish, bland portfolio templates, decorative card grids that hide content, and overdone editorial tropes that make the writing feel staged. Do not turn the site into a corporate resume shell or a novelty demo that makes the actual work harder to inspect.

## Design Principles

- Make the work inspectable: project and post surfaces should expose enough concrete context to reward scanning.
- Keep the personal voice visible: polish should sharpen the writing, not sterilize it.
- Lead with evidence: use dates, tags, excerpts, imagery, and readable prose hierarchy to show substance quickly.
- Preserve the cinematic dark copper identity while keeping contrast and legibility high.
- Prefer useful rhythm over decoration: visual treatments should help visitors choose what to read next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text, keyboard-friendly navigation, descriptive links, useful image alt text where images carry meaning, and reduced-motion-compatible interactions. Blog reading surfaces should preserve comfortable line length and robust overflow handling for tables, code, and images.
